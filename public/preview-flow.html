01

Two photos, one zone

The testimonial and difference sections run directly into each other. Same dark aesthetic, different photo. You scroll through both without hitting a box edge.

02

One light contact section

The statement ("We don't fit families...") and the contact CTA are one section. No separate pause box. The statement is the emotional opener, the CTA is the close.

03

5 zones not 7 boxes

Hero → Intro → Cinematic dark → Contact → Footer. The background only changes 4 times the whole way down. That's what makes it feel like a scroll, not a slideshow.
